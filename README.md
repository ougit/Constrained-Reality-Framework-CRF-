# Constrained Reality Framework (CRF)

**Constrained Reality Framework (CRF)** is a foundational theoretical framework that derives physical reality from the primitive concept of *distinction*.  
It bridges quantum mechanics, information theory, and ontology, and generates falsifiable predictions — most notably the **P0 Hypothesis**:

> Detectors with greater accumulated measurement history collapse quantum states faster.

## Key Testable Prediction — P0 Hypothesis

**Core claim**  
The rate of wavefunction collapse depends on the *total measurement history* (informational depth) of the detecting system.

**Possible experimental directions**  
- Delayed-choice / single-photon interference with detectors of different “age” or complexity  
- Weak measurement + post-selection protocols  
- Correlation experiments comparing “veteran” vs newly initialized detectors

These can be contrasted directly with standard quantum mechanics (no history dependence).
